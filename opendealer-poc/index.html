Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

How does Open Dealer differ from legacy marketplaces? Open Close

Traditional marketplaces keep shoppers on their sites and charge you for the leads they intercept. Open Dealer sends every shopper click directly to your VDPs. You keep the leads, the data, and the customer relationship.

What inventory formats do you support? Open Close

Open Dealer works with CSV, XML, and direct API integrations. It connects to your existing feed, so there are no new processes or logins for your staff.

How does AI distribution work? Open Close

Inventory is transformed into modern JSON APIs indexed for AI assistants. Shoppers using ChatGPT, AI search, or other AI tools can discover your vehicles, and every path leads back to your dealership.

Is there a per-lead or per-click fee? Open Close

No. Open Dealer uses flat, predictable monthly pricing. There are no hidden fees, bidding, or pay-per-click surprises.

How quickly can I get started? Open Close

Most dealerships are live within days. Send your inventory feed and Open Dealer handles the cleaning, enrichment, and distribution.

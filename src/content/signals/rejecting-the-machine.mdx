I’m feeling a heavy rejection of technology in interesting places. Instead of synthetic athleisure clothing, i’m seeking out natural, cotton fibers again. Instead of my tech bags and backpacks, i’m opting for simple totes. I feel myself searching for ways to differentiate myself from machines. And I welcome it.

I think where previously it felt efficient and good to align myself to the benefits of technology, i’m now turning my attention to the benefits of being a human animal.
